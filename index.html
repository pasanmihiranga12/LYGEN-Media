About Us

LYGEN Media Agency, in plain terms.

LYGEN Media Agency is a media agency that builds websites, social media, and content systems for brands that want to look and feel bigger than their budget. We're the media and web division of Lygenex Labs, our parent company, and we're based in Sri Lanka — working with clients both locally and internationally.

End-to-end web design and development, social media management, content creation, custom systems, e-commerce builds, and ongoing maintenance — all under one roof, so you're not juggling five different freelancers.
