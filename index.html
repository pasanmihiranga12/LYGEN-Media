About Us

LYGEN Media Agency, in plain terms.

LYGEN Media Agency is a media agency that builds websites, social media, and content systems for brands that want to look and feel bigger than their budget. We're the media and web division of Lygenex Labs, our parent company, and we're based in Sri Lanka — working with clients both locally and internationally.

What we do

End-to-end web design and development, social media management, content creation, custom systems, e-commerce builds, and ongoing maintenance — all under one roof, so you're not juggling five different freelancers.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is LYGEN Media Agency?

LYGEN Media Agency is a media agency that builds custom websites, social media, and content systems for brands. It's part of Lygenex Labs and based in Sri Lanka.

Is LYGEN Media Agency related to Lygenex Labs?

Yes — LYGEN Media Agency is the media and web division of Lygenex Labs, our parent company.

Where is LYGEN Media Agency located?

We're based in Sri Lanka and work with clients both locally and internationally.

What services does LYGEN offer?

Web design and development, social media management, content creation, custom systems, e-commerce solutions, and ongoing maintenance and optimization.

How much does a website cost?

Packages start at Rs. 45,000 for a Starter site and go up to Rs. 175,000 for a fully custom Scale build. Social media, ads, and content are quoted separately based on scope.

Who founded LYGEN Media Agency?

LYGEN was founded by Pasan Liyanage, who serves as Founder and Creative Director.
